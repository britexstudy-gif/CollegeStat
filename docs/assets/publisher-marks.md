# Publisher identification assets

Publisher marks identify the source of a ranking; they do not imply endorsement.

- QS: official downloadable PNG, https://support.qs.com/hc/en-gb/articles/27713381687836-QS-World-University-Rankings-logo-usage-guidelines, asset https://support.qs.com/hc/en-gb/article_attachments/27713385631004.
- THE: wordmark from https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Times_Higher_Education_logo.svg (PD-textlogo; vector by J. Avanzado), https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/b5/Times_Higher_Education_logo.svg.
- ShanghaiRanking: official site header, https://www.shanghairanking.com/_nuxt/img/logo_white.8aca224.svg.
- CWTS: official Leiden Open site, https://open.leidenranking.com/applepie/media/images/style/logos/cwts_logo_complete_white.png.

Local copies are in `public/logos/publishers`. Logos retain their original proportions and colors. White marks use a contrasting publisher-colored background. Source links accompany ranking entries and score sections.
