# Rival and adjacent product research (2026-11-15)

Scope: ideas only, no copied text. Method: public web search plus public pages; no logins. Search tooling returned thin results for local Telegram/Instagram and Reddit, so local-market and complaint sections lean on news reports and on structural observation. Items marked (K) come from general product knowledge, not a fetched page this session; verify before relying on them.

## 1. Per-rival highlights

**CollegeVine** ([Sage AI counselor](https://www.collegevine.com/sage), [reviews roundup](https://www.findmyorbit.com/blog/collegevine-sage-review-2026))
- Free chancing from GPA, tests, activities; free AI counselor, paid human advising as upsell.
- AI used for list-building, major exploration, essay brainstorming, academic planning.
- Complaint to learn from: chancing reported as optimistic at selective schools and odd on safeties (Trustpilot). Show ranges and caveats, not one number.

**Niche** ([overview](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niche.com))
- Letter-grade "report cards" per college by topic (academics, value, campus life) built from official data plus student reviews.
- Rankings pages by niche (best for X) that feed SEO.
- Scholarship search and direct-admit offers to drive signups.
- Critique: reviews skew to extremes; flag sample size.

**BigFuture (College Board)** (K)
- Clean college search with filters (size, cost, major) and cost/aid calculators; "fit" questionnaire; step-by-step plan checklist.
- Verified-source data presented plainly; deadlines surfaced per college.

**Common App** (K)
- One profile reused across many colleges; per-college requirement checklist with deadlines; clear "what is missing" status.
- Recommender and document status tracking.

**UCAS** ([course search](https://www.ucas.com/explore/search), [Unibuddy chat](https://www.ucas.com/corporate/news-and-key-documents/news/connecting-futures-ucas-and-unibuddys-new-online-chat-matches-students-inside-look-uni-life))
- Course-first search (not university-first), with entry requirements per course.
- Hub account with shortlist and dates; students can chat with matched current-student ambassadors.
- Published yearly key-dates timeline that students plan around.

**Studyportals / Bachelorsportal** ([about](https://www.bachelorsportal.com/about-us))
- Huge programme index, 5,500+ scholarships with provider type, award type, amount, deadline on each programme page.
- Data sourced from official university sites, verified, refreshed at least yearly (stated in FAQ).
- Programme pages combine tuition, duration, language requirement, deadline in one scannable block.

**uni-assist** (K)
- Single gateway for many German universities; document checklist per country of school certificate.
- Country-specific rules (how Kazakh/Uzbek diplomas are assessed) as first-class content.

**Crimson Education** ([KZ blog](https://www.crimsoneducation.org/kz-ru/blog/how-to-apply-for-an-undergraduate-program-abroad-from-kazakhstan))
- Localised, Russian-language, country-specific guides for Kazakh students (shows the SEO keyword space is already contested).
- Positions around a roadmap/timeline plus mentors; high-priced human service.

**Unibuddy** ([UCAS peer chat](https://www.ucas.com/providers/our-products-and-services/student-recruitment-and-marketing/peer-peer-engagement))
- Peer ambassadors answer questions, searchable and filterable by course/country.
- Trust mechanic: a real student, not an admissions office.

**Shorelight** (K)
- Pathway/bridge programs with guaranteed progression; bundles visa guidance and living support.
- Frames "conditional offer to full admission" clearly.

**ApplyBoard** ([Edinburgh lists it as an agent in KZ](https://www.ed.ac.uk/studying/international/agents/list/kazakhstan))
- Matches student profile to programs with real-time eligibility; single application, status tracker.
- Agent/university registry shows which intermediaries universities officially use, a trust signal worth mirroring.

**Leverage Edu** (K)
- AI-assisted course/university finder plus counselling funnel; heavy content marketing (guides per country/exam).

**Kollegio** ([site](https://kollegio.ai), [funding news](https://siliconangle.com/2025/04/23/startup-kollegio-raises-2-8m-ai-powered-college-counseling-service))
- Free-to-start AI counseling: list recommender, essay feedback, scholarship tracking, deadlines.
- Raised seed money on an access/equity story; US-centric, so international coverage is the gap.

**Scoir / Naviance** ([Scoir scattergrams](https://scoir.helpdocs.io/article/9dstwx6bb4-using-scattergrams-to-assess-admissions-probability), [study on effect](https://hechingerreport.org/proof-points-two-studies-find-scattergrams-reduce-applications-to-elite-colleges/))
- Scattergrams: GPA/test dots of past applicants (accepted/denied/waitlisted) with your position overlaid.
- Research found scattergrams can reduce applications to elite colleges, so use carefully (can discourage under-matching students).
- School-level data; students self-report outcomes.

**Bluebook (College Board)** (K)
- Realistic exam-environment practice with timer, tools, review of mistakes; adaptive modules.

**IELTS prep apps (British Council / IDP)** (K)
- Official-style practice tests, band estimation, speaking/writing feedback; test-date and fee info.

**Duolingo English Test** (K)
- Cheap, at-home, quick result; widely accepted list per university is a key content type.

**Medium (reading UX)** (K)
- Distraction-free article layout, reading time, highlights, clean typography, related-read flow.

**OnePrep (college section)**: not fetched this session; direct review of their public pages is a to-do.

## 3. Best onboarding question set (adapted)

Order seen to work: easy identity facts first, constraints next, preferences last, then show value before asking for more.
1. Country and city; grade you are in now (9-12 / gap year). Sets timeline.
2. Study language and school type (national, NIS/BIS/IB, lyceum). Sets transcript handling.
3. Grades: system used (5-point, 100-point, GPA) and current average. Normalised under the hood.
4. Exams taken or planned: IELTS/TOEFL/DET/SAT/none, scores and dates.
5. Intended major (or "not sure", with 3 interest picks).
6. Target destinations (multi-select: US, UK, Germany, Korea, Turkey, Canada, Europe, Asia).
7. Budget per year and need for full aid (slider: can pay / partial / only full scholarship).
8. Biggest worry (money, English, visa, essays, picking a school). Drives first content shown.
9. Optional: activities, awards, olympiads (defer; ask later in profile).
10. Contact: Telegram handle or email, only after first shortlist appears.

## 4. SEO and AI-search tactics to copy (10)

1. Programmatic programme/university pages with a consistent data block (Studyportals pattern).
2. "Best for X" ranking pages by narrow niche (Niche pattern), e.g. "cheapest English-taught bachelor's in Europe".
3. Country-of-origin landing pages ("Study abroad from Uzbekistan") in three languages with hreflang (Crimson does it for KZ).
4. Scholarship hub pages with filterable cards and deadline-sorted "closing soon" lists.
5. Structured data: EducationalOrganization/CollegeOrUniversity, Course, FAQPage, Article, BreadcrumbList, Dataset for tuition tables.
6. Visible "last updated" and source citations; AI answer engines favour dated, cited, quotable facts.
7. Short answer-first paragraphs and Q&A blocks (TL;DR on top) that LLMs can quote.
8. Exam-acceptance matrix pages ("which universities accept DET") as comparison tables.
9. Yearly deadline calendars and "2027 intake" evergreen URLs updated, not recreated.
10. Publish an llms.txt / clean sitemap and open, crawlable HTML (no gated content), plus a public data page agents can cite.

## 5. Gaps nobody fills for Central Asian students

- Grade and diploma conversion from Kazakh/Uzbek/Kyrgyz/Tajik systems to each destination's requirements, in Russian and local languages.
- Honest chancing for students without US-style GPA or extracurricular norms (olympiads, republic competitions).
- Total-cost and proof-of-funds view in local currency (KZT, UZS, KGS, TJS) and realistic family-income framing.
- Non-US/UK destinations (Korea, Turkey, Germany, Poland, Hungary, UAE) beside US/UK in one place.
- Verified-consultant directory or scam-check; regulators in Uzbekistan have named fraudulent consultancies ([Kun.uz](https://kun.uz/en/news/2025/06/19/new-law-aims-to-regulate-labor-migration-amid-wave-of-consultant-fraud)).
- Kazakh-student stories exist as media articles ([WeProject](https://www.weproject.media/articles/detail/ya-ne-sdaval-ent-kazakhstantsy-o-tom-kak-srazu-postupit-v-universitet-za-rubezhom/)), but no structured, comparable peer outcome data.
- Telegram-native experience: reminders, bot Q&A, shareable cards for parents.
- Local-payment-friendly pricing, and no dependence on foreign cards.

## Caveats

- OnePrep, Bluebook, IELTS apps, Duolingo, uni-assist, Shorelight, Leverage, Medium bullets are from general knowledge; confirm before citing.
