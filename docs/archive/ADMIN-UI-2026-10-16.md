# Staff workspace refinement — 16 October 2026

The staff console uses a neutral black base, blue actions and white text in dark mode, with white surfaces and the same blue accent in light mode. Typography follows the shared Inter family. Large blue or navy panels and the decorative marketing banner have been removed from the overview.

The overview now starts with live catalogue counts, followed by the evidence review queue, university profile editing shortcuts, a source-to-publication workflow, and recorded account activity. Missing metrics remain unavailable; no fabricated charts or activity counts have been added. Navigation keeps the four main areas and searchable section links, without visible category headings dividing the links.

`components/college/admin-overview.tsx` contains presentation only. The existing staff page retains authorization, the verified session boundary, request cancellation, account ownership checks, live reads, and mutation handlers. Column selection, record drawers, imports, and review decisions retain their existing behavior. Empty tables and loading states now include consistent icons; reduced-motion preferences disable the loading spinner animation.

Native Inspo references studied:

- [Feedly](https://feedly.com) — `feedly-com--i-top-bu-blogs`, captured 13 May 2026: persistent navigation and clear separation between functional records and supporting controls. Composition informed the structure; its green palette was not adopted.
- [Ghost](https://ghost.org) — `ghost-org--creators`, captured 13 May 2026: restrained sans-serif hierarchy and neutral surfaces. The shared Inter system takes precedence over mixed display typography.
