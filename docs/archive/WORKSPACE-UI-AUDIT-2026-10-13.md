# Workspace and staff UI audit — 13 October 2026

The student workspace is usable and has a coherent purpose: research universities, preserve private work, and turn application preparation into tasks. The writing desk and the staff support inbox are the strongest complete screens. The main weaknesses are navigation ambiguity around the Library, lengthy phone layouts, and staff tables that expose database IDs before useful names. The current student experience is around **7/10**; the staff experience is around **6.8/10**. These are design judgments, not a claim that the live account, database, or AI services have been certified.

Keep the four student destinations **Home / Universities / Library / My profile**, and the four staff areas **Overview / Catalogue / Review / Operations**. The next improvement should refine the work inside those areas rather than add more destinations.

## Evidence and limits

Fresh screenshots come from the one local preview at `http://127.0.0.1:5173`. Authenticated screens use synthetic student and administrator identities, invented records explicitly named “Synthetic,” and synthetic metadata. Every Supabase request and every account or staff API write from those contexts was intercepted. No real credentials, sign-in, password reset, hosted private record, AI request, or save was used. The anonymous staff gate is an actual anonymous local screen. Screenshot fixtures demonstrate the application's rendered interface; they do not establish live authentication, provider reliability, policy correctness, or production completeness.

The original capture, focused follow-up, correction verification, and final checks have separate receipts in `outputs/ui-audit-oct13/workspace/`. The index contains **99 unique capture entries: 93 current reviewed captures, 3 before-correction proofs, and 3 initial fixture captures excluded from ratings**, plus the actual anonymous staff gate. An initial scholarship fixture omitted its nested links, and an initial MCP fixture returned an empty notification response. Those caused fixture-only failures. The fixture shapes were corrected, fresh `validated` screenshots were captured, and neither failure is counted as a product defect. A later navigation timeout and selector failures are retained as automation receipts and also excluded from the rating. The original preview also emitted repeated “send was called before connect” errors; these did not recur in the three focused final receipts. Their origin was not established, so they are retained as a preview/runtime limitation rather than attributed to a product screen.

The actual local student AI page reports **“Live AI is currently unavailable.”** Its four mode screens were reviewed, but live feedback and provider failures were not exercised. The staff MCP “Connected” screenshot reflects a synthetic protocol handshake. It does not establish that live AI is connected. The supplied operating context says the staff ledger, TOTP verification, 15-minute idle boundary, support inbox, and structure tool are installed, while AI keys are disconnected; the shared role helper remains unchanged by owner choice. This review does not reopen that backend decision.

Desktop captures are 1440 × 1000. Phone captures are 390 × 844. Additional 330- and 768-pixel checks are recorded for principal workspace and staff families. All sampled principal routes fit their page width. Staff tables still require horizontal scrolling inside the table: fitting the page is weaker evidence than making the task comfortable on a phone. Screenshot contexts request reduced motion; running animations were zero in captured settled states. Motion scores are provisional, because a full normal-motion and screen-reader audit is outside this pass.

## Rating rubric

Each dimension uses 0–10: 0–2 absent or seriously obstructed; 3–4 substantial task friction; 5–6 workable with obvious gaps; 7–8 good with targeted improvements; 9–10 exceptionally clear and well evidenced. The overall family score is the unweighted average of usefulness, navigation, visual hierarchy, responsiveness, trust, and motion. “Trust” rates the clarity of sources, state, privacy, and limits in the interface; it is not a security certification. A disconnected feature loses usefulness even when its explanation is honest.

## Corrected defect and retained evidence

The priorities page's country-list checkboxes contained absolute hidden inputs whose containing block allowed their positions to extend beyond the clipped country list. Meaningful content ended around 1432 desktop pixels, while the document remained scrollable to 5173. At 390 pixels, document height reached 9946. This was a product defect rather than a synthetic record problem.

The minimal correction gives each country checkbox label a relative containing block. The independent local browser check measured **5173 → 1432** desktop pixels and **9946 → 1892** phone pixels. The Andorra checkbox still toggled **false → true → false** with the Space key. No Save was pressed and the fixture intercepted all account writes. Current profile scores use the corrected layout. The larger design improvements remain pending.
