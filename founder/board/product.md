# Product lens
Score: 5/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **It is live.** collegestat.xyz is up, indexed and has working sign-in. Last review's "ship it" condition is met. Strangers can now have the first five minutes.
- **The form practice is a real product moment.** The student fills in the portal each university actually uses (Common App, UCAS, uni-assist and others), with the real sections and limits, and ends with a decision letter that gives reasons. It starts from the experience, and no general search site offers it.
- **Craft and honesty still hold.** Every figure cites its source and year. The FAQ says chances are "an estimate, not a promise". The AI will not write the essay. The 30 guides now also exist in Russian and Uzbek, the languages this student reads.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **It widened again instead of narrowing.** Last review asked for cuts. Since then the product has added an AI agent with tools, a paywall, a plan calendar, a downloadable report, sticker avatars, a first-run tour and a longer onboarding. The live landing page still sells search, chances, essays, forms, scholarships, test practice, 14,003 universities and six languages. Each addition is well made, but together they still don't name one thing.
2. **The seams contradict each other.** The brief says Pro costs $9/month. The live site shows three tiers: Explorer $0, Scholar $6/month ($39/year) and Admit $15/month ($99/year). Scholar is free during early access. The founder, the board and the buyer are reading different offers. The work built 18–20 Nov is not deployed, so the live experience is not the one being reviewed either.
3. **The core answers may still be missing on the target pages.** The brief reports no new coverage numbers for deadlines, tuition or IELTS across the 204 targets. The last figures were 44%, 38% and 22%, and the 50-value import has not been applied. Chances, plan and decision letter all rest on those numbers. When the data is thin, the best moment in the product, the decision letter, gives a confident answer on an empty base. Central Asia-specific scholarships are still at zero.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- In one sentence, what does a student in Tashkent get here that they cannot get anywhere else?
- Which is the true price: $9 Pro, or $6/$15 Scholar/Admit? Who decided, and for which payer?
- Since 15 Nov, has any real student completed onboarding? Where did they stop?
- What is coverage across the 204 targets today, after the import?
- What does the decision letter say when deadline, tuition or IELTS is unknown?
- Who reads the Spanish, German and Chinese versions?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. **Pick the one thing and cut to it.** For example: "Practise your real application to the universities you can afford, and see what to fix." Lead the landing page with it. Move everything else behind it or remove it.
2. **Make every touchpoint tell one story.** Keep one price table, matching across the brief, the landing page, the FAQ and the paywall. Deploy the built work or drop it.
3. **Finish the data slice.** Apply the import. Reach at least 90% coverage of deadlines, tuition and IELTS across the 204, or shrink the list. Wherever data is missing, show "not verified" and never give a verdict.
4. **Watch 10 students from the segment** use the live site. Freeze new features until what breaks for them is fixed.
