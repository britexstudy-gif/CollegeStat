# Board brief: CollegeStat

## The idea

- What it is: an online university-preparation platform: search and compare 14,000+ universities (rankings, admissions, costs, programs, scholarships, deadlines, all with cited sources), plus test practice (SAT, IELTS, TOEFL style), an essay/writing desk, an application plan and an AI advisor, all driven by one student profile.
- Who it is for: high-school students (and their families) preparing to apply to universities, including internationally. No narrower segment (country, income level, school year) has been chosen yet.
- What it sells, at what price: Free tier (search, rankings, scholarships, plan, question bank, 1 mock per exam, writing checks, 5 AI requests/day). Pro at $9/month or $5.40/month billed yearly (all full-length mocks with score history, unlimited AI essay review and coaching, admission simulator, personal study plan). Payments are not connected; during early access Pro is free and "Upgrade" joins a waitlist.
- Where and how: web app. Not yet in production hosting; runs locally, a hosted database has been tested. No acquisition channel has been chosen.
- Budget and constraints: solo founder building with AI coding agents. Budget, revenue goals and launch date not stated. Users and revenue: none yet.

## Additional context from the project

- The founder's own product brief describes the vision as "Discover → Research → Compare → Prepare → Build Their Profile → Apply → Get Accepted → Share Their Experience", organised into 8 sections: Dashboard, Universities, Academics (test prep), Applications (simulators, essays, deadlines, scholarships), Community (accepted students, reviews), Knowledge Hub, AI Advisor, My Profile. Core principle: "a student should enter their information once, and CollegeStat should use it everywhere."
- Built so far (per the project README): search/filter by country, subject, cost, admission criteria; university profiles with sections for applying, programs, costs, rankings, research, campus, gallery and sources; comparison of up to 5 universities with charts and CSV export; scholarships (7 awards and 20 application dates installed so far); original SAT-style and IELTS-style practice exercises (explicitly not official tests or score predictions); writing desk with guides for personal statements, motivation letters and recommendation requests; private saved plans and essay version history; staff admin console.
- A strong emphasis on data honesty: every figure keeps its source, year and currency; unknown values are never shown as zero.
- Development history: about one month of intensive building (late September to October 2026), dozens of internal checkpoint and audit documents, much of the work done by AI coding agents from written prompts.
- Not done: production hosting, payments, email/OAuth delivery, any marketing or user acquisition, any user research.
