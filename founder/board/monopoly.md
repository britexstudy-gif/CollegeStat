# Monopoly lens
Score: 4/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **It is live and indexed.** collegestat.xyz runs and Google, Bing and Yandex have the sitemaps. A low-cost, compounding channel has started.
- **Native-language content is the best wedge on the board.** 30 guides in Russian and Uzbek plus verified facts for 204 targets start a search footprint for this segment. Whether rivals already rank there is unknown.
- **The portal-replica simulator is the closest thing to proprietary know-how.** Practising in each university's real portal is harder to copy than a list of facts. Whether it is ten times better than what applicants use now is untested.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **Still no distribution.** No users or traffic have been reported, no channel beyond SEO is chosen, and most new work is not deployed. SEO is slow, and the 2027 intake has fixed deadlines.
2. **Breadth grew again.** The last review asked to freeze features. Since then came an assistant with tools, a desk redesign, a new dashboard, a calendar, an AI report, longer onboarding, sticker avatars and a tour, each a crowded front with near-zero share. Meanwhile the data where dominance looked possible is still partial (deadlines 89, tuition 77, IELTS 44 of 204), and 50 verified values sit unapplied.
3. **No secret, no network effect.** Public data can be re-collected, the AI is a third-party model anyone can call, and nothing improves with each user. A local agency or funded platform can match it.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- How many people have visited, signed up and come back since 15 Nov? (Not reported.)
- Who serves this student today (agencies, Telegram channels, counsellors), and at what price? (Unknown; /founder-competitors.)
- What do you know about Central Asian applicants that those players do not? One sentence.
- How do the first 100 students arrive, and what does each cost?
- Which one of the portal simulator, the verified data or the native-language guides would you keep if you had to cut the rest?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. **Deploy, then measure.** Ship the built work, apply the import, report visits, sign-ups and returning users by source.
2. **Pick one channel beyond SEO** where these students already gather, and get the first 100 users through it.
3. **Freeze features for real.** Finish coverage for the 204 targets and find Central-Asia-specific scholarships.
4. **Start the network effect now.** Collect this cohort's applications and results (who applied where, who got in) for the next cohort.
5. **Write the secret down**.
