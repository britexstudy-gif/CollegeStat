# Offers lens
Score: 4/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **The market still holds up.** Central Asian students applying abroad for the 2027 intake feel real pain, and the deadline gives urgency that does not have to be faked.
- **The stack moved closer to an outcome.** The practice application in each university's real portal, ending in a decision letter with reasons, is the first feature that looks like a result rather than research. It cuts effort and fear on the hardest step.
- **A paywall moment now exists.** Running out of daily AI requests opens an upgrade screen at a point of felt need. That is the right place to test willingness to pay.
- **One condition from last time is met.** The app has been live at collegestat.xyz since 15 Nov.
- **Delivery stays cheap**, so a guarantee and bonuses remain affordable.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **Likelihood is still zero.** There are no users, no revenue and no result anyone has achieved. The paywall leads to a waitlist, not a payment. Nothing yet shows that a buyer believes this gets them admitted or funded, and the value equation fails on that.
2. **The offer and price are unchanged for the third review.** Pro is still $9/month and still a list of features (full mocks, unlimited AI review, simulator, study plan). It has no named result, no guarantee and no season price. The payer is still undecided, and a monthly plan in a seasonal business invites people to cancel after deadlines.
3. **Features keep growing instead of the offer.** The board asked for a feature freeze until there was paid proof. Instead, 18-20 Nov added the portal rebuild, writing desk, AI tools, dashboard, onboarding, stickers and a tour, and most of it is not deployed. The data the offer would rest on (tuition, foundation years, scholarships aimed at Central Asian students) still has gaps.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- Who pays, the parent or the student? What do they pay a local counsellor or prep centre today? (/founder-consumer, /founder-competitors)
- What one result, by what date, would you put your name on for 2027?
- How many people have hit the AI paywall, and how many joined the waitlist? If you do not know, why not?
- Could you take real money from 5 families this month, by hand if needed, before connecting payments?
- What guarantee could you afford? (/founder-offer, /founder-cfo)
- Would you still be the obvious choice at double the price? If not, what is missing?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. **Name one offer tied to the 2027 deadline.** For example: an affordable shortlist with deadlines, scholarships and a practice application for each school. Stack only what serves it, and add a guarantee the CFO approves. (/founder-offer)
2. **Choose the payer and test a season price** against what a human counsellor costs, not against free tools. (/founder-pricing)
3. **Freeze features, deploy what is built and collect proof.** Get at least 5 paying families and one measurable result each. Report the paywall and waitlist numbers to the board.
4. **Fill the data the promise depends on.** Get tuition and IELTS minimums for every school a named offer would include.
