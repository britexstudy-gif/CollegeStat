## What would flip a no

- Several parents I know in the Tashkent study-abroad Telegram chats saying their child used it last season and it was accurate, plus payment by Click/Payme and a named team I can message directly.
- A real parent I know, from my city, telling me their child used it and it caught a mistake the agency missed - plus being able to pay locally (Kaspi/Click/Payme) instead of an international card.
- If the agency's shortlist turned out to be only the universities that pay them commission, and a parent I know said this service found real scholarships for their child, and I could pay locally in sums through Click or Payme.
- Payment by Click, Payme or Kaspi plus a named Russian-speaking person who manages the deadlines with my child and calls me when something is missing.
- Several parents in my Telegram groups saying their child used it for Germany or Korea and the shortlist and uni-assist practice were accurate, plus a lower price or paying in local currency without a foreign card.
- If the free practice application caught a real mistake or missing item that ChatGPT and our own checking had missed, or if the essay reviewers had verifiable admissions or top-university credentials.
- If another parent I know, or my child's school, told me it caught a mistake the agency made, or a cheaper first payment so I can try it before giving the full $99.
- A cheaper plan around $25-30 only for Germany/Poland/Korea/Turkey, after the free version shows me correct deadlines and costs for universities I already checked myself.
- If I could pay by Click or Payme in sums and someone I know, or my child's school, said they used it and it caught a mistake the agency made.
- Someone I know personally, like my cousin's family or a parent from my child's school, telling me they used it and it worked, and being able to talk to a real person in Russian or Uzbek before I pay.
- A parent I know personally, or the school, telling me their child used it and it worked, plus a real phone number or office in Tashkent I can visit. Then I might try the free part first.
- Real results from families like mine: names of students from Tashkent or Almaty who got into strong universities with this, and who exactly does the essay review.
