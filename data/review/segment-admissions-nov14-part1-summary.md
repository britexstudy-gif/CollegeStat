# Admissions research segment, part 1 — 14 November 2026

Scope: target indexes 6–54 inclusive (0-based), 49 universities, in target-file order. Official university admissions pages were fetched for all 49; up to three relevant same-domain pages were fetched where discoverable from those pages. The sources list records official page URLs and checked page excerpts or research notes. No third-party sources were used.

Total official page records captured: 146 before field-specific follow-up checks. Verified values remain marked `staged` and include their own `source_url` and `checked_at`. Other fields remain null where the official pages reviewed did not clearly establish a fact for international first-year bachelor applicants and the requested 2027 cycle or a dated latest cycle.

## Notable gaps

- Annual international tuition is often programme-specific; the TUM page states semester tuition bands for non-EU/EEA bachelor's students beginning in winter 2027/28, not a single annual price.
- U.S. deadlines and English tests were not captured in a current, explicitly dated cycle for most institutions. The UCI application window is explicitly labelled fall 2027.
- UK institutions often route undergraduate applications through UCAS, but generic or UK-applicant-only statements were not treated as evidence about international applicants unless the university page made that scope clear.
- German admission routes, language thresholds, qualification recognition and foundation pathways are programme- and certificate-specific; no country-level assumptions were entered.
