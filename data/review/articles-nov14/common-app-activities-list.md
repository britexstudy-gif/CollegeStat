## In short

- Include meaningful activities, work, interests and family responsibilities.
- Give clear dates, roles and details; be honest about your contribution.
- The 2026–27 Common App calls this section “activities and experiences.”

## Choose what to include

Common App says this part helps colleges understand your life outside class. Examples include clubs, arts, sports, work, volunteering, hobbies and community engagement. The 2026–27 application calls it “activities and experiences.” Check the live form because labels and questions can change by cycle. [Common App application guide](https://www.commonapp.org/apply/first-year-students) [2026–27 changes](https://www.commonapp.org/blog/common-app-opens-application-launch-2026-27-season/)

You do not need a famous award or a long list. Include activities that matter to you and that you can describe truthfully. Family duties, helping at home, paid work or caring responsibilities can show how you use your time. Follow the exact fields and character limit in the current form.

For each item, note what you did, when, how often, and what changed because of your work. Use clear action words and numbers only when you know they are correct. Do not inflate a title, hours or result. Common App offers a first-year toolkit with help for the section. [First-year toolkit](https://www.commonapp.org/apply/fy-toolkit/)

## What to do next

- [ ] List your activities, jobs, interests and responsibilities.
- [ ] Check dates and hours with calendars or records.
- [ ] Choose the items that best show your time and interests.
- [ ] Review the current form limits before entering your descriptions.
