## In short

- The Chinese Government Scholarship has different application routes.
- The call tells you which degree levels and fields are open.
- Confirm undergraduate eligibility with the receiving body and university.

## Find the right route

The China Scholarship Council uses the official Campus China portal for scholarship information and applications. Calls may use different routes and rules. The application guide explains how to choose the programme type and receiving institution code. Follow the guide for the route named in your current call. [Campus China](https://www.campuschina.org/) [Application guide](https://www.campuschina.org/Upload/file/20240408/20240408111909_5912.pdf)

Do not assume that every scholarship call accepts new undergraduate students. Check the current notice for degree level, eligible country, age and education rules, language, deadline, and required papers. Check the exact university programme too. Ask whether the course teaches in Chinese or English and whether you need a language certificate or preparatory year.

Prepare the records named in the notice. These may include identity, school papers, grades, a study plan, language proof, and health forms. The current application system and the receiving organisation decide the exact list. Do not rely on an old checklist or an agent’s promise of selection.

## What to do next

- [ ] Find the open call for your country and degree level.
- [ ] Ask the university whether it admits scholarship applicants to your course.
- [ ] Check the route, code, language, deadline, and documents.
- [ ] Save a copy of the official call and submitted application.
