## In short

- Tuition changes by country, language, legal status and course.
- A scholarship may cover some costs, but check its current call.
- Compare living costs and language needs with tuition.

## Poland

Poland’s official study portal says some full-time programmes taught in Polish at public universities have no tuition for students who meet legal conditions. Foreign-language programmes and many other cases can have fees. Universities set programme prices. Check your status and the exact course price. [Study.gov.pl](https://study.gov.pl/tuition-fees)

## Czechia

The official Study in Czechia guide says public and state universities do not charge tuition for programmes taught in Czech, regardless of nationality. Foreign-language programmes may charge fees. The amount depends on the university and course. Check the current course page and the costs of learning Czech before choosing this route. [Study in Czechia guide](https://www.studyin.cz/soubory/clanky/0021_publications/Publikace-Czech-Higher-education-aktualizace-2024-II-web.pdf)

## Hungary

Stipendium Hungaricum is a scholarship route with an annual call and eligible countries, fields and institutions. The current call sets the terms. Do not assume that every course or student from Central Asia is eligible. Check the scholarship list and the university’s own admission rules. [Official scholarship site](https://stipendiumhungaricum.hu/)

In all three countries, budget for housing, food, travel, insurance, residence steps, and language preparation. A tuition waiver does not automatically pay these costs.

## What to do next

- [ ] Choose the teaching language you can use or learn.
- [ ] Check tuition for the exact course and your legal status.
- [ ] Check scholarship eligibility and what it covers.
- [ ] Build a full yearly budget, including living costs.
