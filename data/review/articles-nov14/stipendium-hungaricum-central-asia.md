# Stipendium Hungaricum for students from Central Asia

## In short

- You apply in two places: the Stipendium Hungaricum online system and through your country's sending partner.
- A portal submission alone does not replace nomination by the sending partner.
- Your national office may set an earlier deadline than the programme's.
- Read the call for the stipend, housing, tuition and insurance terms. They can change between calls.

## Eligibility depends on your sending partner

The 2026–27 call lists Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan and Uzbekistan among eligible sending partners for full-time study. The programme is based on bilateral agreements, and the call says candidates must be nominated by the responsible authority of their sending partner. Check your country's latest partner entry and your national office's process. [2026–27 call](https://stipendiumhungaricum.hu/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/BA_MA_OTM_Call_for_Applications_2026_27.pdf) [Partners](https://stipendiumhungaricum.hu/partners/)

## Two tracks

1. **The online system.** Create an account and choose only study fields open to students from your country under the agreement.
2. **Your sending partner.** Follow its instructions for documents, nomination and any country-specific tests or earlier deadlines.

The programme's application guide describes the online process. [Application guide 2026–27](https://stipendiumhungaricum.hu/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Application_Guide_for_the_Stipendium_Hungaricum_Programme_2026_27_final.pdf) [Apply](https://stipendiumhungaricum.hu/apply/)

Typical documents: passport, school-leaving certificate or proof of expected graduation, transcripts, motivation materials, language proof and course-specific items. The exact list depends on the degree and university. Translate and authenticate records in the format the call, the university and your sending partner request.

> **Example.** Aigerim in Almaty and Sardor in Samarkand both start in the online system, but each also contacts the national office in their own country. Aigerim learns her office wants documents earlier than the portal's own cut-off, so she prepares translated records first.

## What the award covers

The call describes the stipend, housing contribution, tuition coverage and insurance. Read those terms closely and plan a personal budget for costs beyond them. Terms in the 2026–27 call apply to 2026–27 only. [2026–27 call](https://stipendiumhungaricum.hu/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/BA_MA_OTM_Call_for_Applications_2026_27.pdf)

## The usual calendar

Every call sets its own dates, so treat this as a pattern, not a promise. In recent rounds the online system opened in the autumn and the main application deadline fell in mid-January. Sending partners then reviewed and nominated candidates, universities held entrance exams or interviews (often online) in the spring, and final results came in early summer, with studies starting in September. Your national office may close earlier than the portal. Build your own calendar from the live call and your office's notice.

## Choosing programmes

You can usually mark more than one programme in the online system, in order of preference. Use that well:

- Choose programmes you actually want, at universities whose entry rules you meet.
- Read each programme's entrance-exam description. Many test maths, physics, biology or English, depending on the field.
- Mix one very competitive programme with others where you are a strong match.
- Check the language of instruction. Some programmes are taught in English, others in Hungarian after a preparatory year.

## Writing the motivation letter

Universities read many letters that say "Hungary is a beautiful country in the heart of Europe". Yours should explain why *this programme* fits *your plans*: which modules or labs interest you, what you have already done in the field, and what you want to do after graduating. One concrete paragraph about your school project or olympiad work beats three paragraphs about the country.

## Costs the award may not cover

Plan your own money for the first weeks: the visa or residence permit, travel to Hungary if not reimbursed, a deposit for housing, a laptop and textbooks, and phone and transport. Ask current students from your country, through the university's international office, what their first month really cost.

## Before you submit

- Confirm your country is eligible for your degree level and field.
- Find your sending partner's authority and its domestic deadline.
- Check whether the university needs an entrance exam or a separate application.
- Save proof of both the portal submission and national nomination.
