## In short

- MEXT has an undergraduate scholarship route through Japanese embassies.
- The current call and its guide set the rules.
- Ask the Japanese embassy in your country for local dates and steps.

## What the scholarship route involves

Japan’s Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology (MEXT) publishes an undergraduate scholarship call. The 2027 guide is for study at Japanese national universities. It lists fields and majors, eligibility rules, application papers, and selection steps. Read the guide before you prepare an application. [MEXT 2027 guidelines](https://www.mext.go.jp/content/20260420-mxt-kotokoku01-000049232_02.pdf)

MEXT says applicants apply through a Japanese embassy or consulate in their country, or through a Japanese university for routes that use university recommendation. The steps and calendar can differ by route and country. Contact the embassy that serves your place of residence. Do not use another country’s dates as your own. [MEXT application process](https://www.mext.go.jp/a_menu/koutou/ryugaku/boshu/1330944.htm)

The application asks for official forms and supporting records. Follow the current guide for age, school history, fields, exams, health form, and documents. The embassy explains how and where to submit. Do not assume that meeting the basic rules means you will be selected.

## What to do next

- [ ] Open the current undergraduate guide from MEXT.
- [ ] Find the Japanese embassy page for your country.
