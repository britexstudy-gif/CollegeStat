## In short

- Both countries have different rules for each university and course.
- Malaysia uses EMGS for many student-pass steps; check current fees and insurance.
- Singapore costs and grants depend on the institution and the offer.

## Compare the course and total cost

For Malaysia, the MYCAS portal lets international applicants explore courses and apply to participating institutions. Check the university’s own admission rules, course price, and English requirements too. Education Malaysia’s fee calculator lists student-pass-related charges. It says these do not include tuition or other university fees, and insurance cost can vary. [MYCAS](https://mycas.educationmalaysia.gov.my/faq.html/) [EMGS fee calculator](https://visa.educationmalaysia.gov.my/catalog/product/view/id/7568/category/123)

For Singapore, apply through each institution’s official admissions route. Read the Immigration and Checkpoints Authority page for the Student’s Pass. If you are offered a Tuition Grant, read the Ministry of Education rules and any work obligation before accepting. Do not count a grant until the university confirms your offer and terms. [ICA Student’s Pass](https://www.ica.gov.sg/reside/STP/apply) [MOE Tuition Grant](https://www.moe.gov.sg/financial-matters/tuition-grant-scheme)

Scholarships, fees and places can change each year. Look for the current call and ask the university how it treats applicants from Uzbekistan, Kazakhstan, and your country of residence. Plan for housing, travel, insurance, visa costs and personal expenses as well as tuition.

## What to do next

- [ ] Pick courses and check their official admission pages.
- [ ] Ask whether the course is open to your citizenship and school certificate.
- [ ] Compare tuition, housing, pass fees, insurance and confirmed aid.
- [ ] Read all scholarship or grant conditions before you accept.
