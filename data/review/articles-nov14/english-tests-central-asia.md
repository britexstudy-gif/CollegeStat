# IELTS, TOEFL or Duolingo: which English test and where to take it

## In short

- Do not pick a test first. Pick your universities first, then read what each one accepts.
- UK visa rules can require an approved test taken at an official centre, even when the university accepts an at-home test.
- A city appearing on a locator does not mean a seat exists on your date. Search the date, not just the city.
- TOEFL iBT changed its score scale in January 2026, and IELTS is moving away from paper tests. Read requirements on the current scale.

## Choose from your university list

IELTS Academic, TOEFL iBT and the Duolingo English Test are not interchangeable by default. A university may accept all three, some, or require one test for one course or visa route. On each admissions page, note the overall and section scores, how old a score may be, whether online tests are allowed and whether scores must be sent directly. Duolingo's university directory shows which institutions accept it. UCAS notes that English requirements are set by the university. [Duolingo universities](https://englishtest.duolingo.com/institutions) [UCAS English requirements](https://www.ucas.com/international/international-students/applying-to-university-as-an-international-student/entry-requirements-for-uk-courses)

A simple way to organise this is one line per university with five notes: tests accepted, overall score, lowest section score, at-home allowed (yes or no), and how scores are sent. Once you have that list, the right test is usually obvious: it is the one accepted by every university on your list, or by the most important ones.

## UK visa caution

For a UK Student visa at degree level, GOV.UK says your English must be equivalent to CEFR level B2, and that a university can assess your English itself, possibly with a different test, as long as it is still equivalent to B2. If you have no qualification that proves your English, you need a Secure English Language Test (SELT) from an approved provider. [GOV.UK: knowledge of English](https://www.gov.uk/student-visa/knowledge-of-english)

The approved SELTs include IELTS for UKVI, Pearson PTE Academic UKVI and LanguageCert SELT tests, among others. They must be taken at an approved test location, and the result must have been awarded in the two years before your visa application. The Duolingo English Test and TOEFL iBT are not on that list. [GOV.UK: approved SELTs](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/prove-your-english-language-abilities-with-a-secure-english-language-test-selt)

IELTS also says tests taken for a UKVI visa must be taken at an official IELTS centre. A university accepting IELTS Online or another at-home test does not mean the visa authority accepts it. [IELTS test modes](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/why-choose-ielts/ways-to-take-ielts)

What this means in practice: before you book, ask each UK university in writing, "Will you assess my English for the visa using the test I plan to take, or do I need a UKVI SELT?" Keep the reply.

## Finding a seat in Central Asia

Use the official IELTS locator and filter by country and test type. When checked on 14 November 2026 it listed British Council centres in Tashkent and Bishkek, an IDP centre in Almaty and Edu Action in Dushanbe. Formats, fees and dates vary and change, so open the city page and check the date before you book. If you need IELTS for UKVI, check that the centre offers that exact test, not only IELTS Academic. [Tashkent](https://ielts.org/test-centres/british-council-american-university-of-technology) [Almaty](https://ielts.org/test-centres/idp-ielts-kazakhstan-almaty-test-room) [Bishkek](https://ielts.org/test-centres/british-council-academia-education-center) [Dushanbe](https://ielts.org/test-centres/edu-action-dushanbe) [IELTS locator](https://ielts.org/test-centres)

ETS lists Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan and Uzbekistan as TOEFL iBT countries. Its scheduler shows the latest dates and in-person seats by city, and the country list alone does not guarantee a seat. [ETS country list](https://www.ets.org/toefl/test-takers/ibt/where-to-test/country-list.html) [ETS scheduling](https://www.ets.org/toefl/test-takers/ibt/schedule.html)

GOV.UK advises booking a SELT through the provider's official website, and warns that you may have to travel to another country if there is no approved centre near you. Do not pay an agent for a "guaranteed" seat. [GOV.UK: approved SELTs](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/prove-your-english-language-abilities-with-a-secure-english-language-test-selt)

## The three tests side by side

- **IELTS Academic.** Four parts: listening, reading, writing and speaking, scored on a 0–9 band scale. Speaking is a face-to-face conversation with an examiner. In March 2026 IELTS announced that from mid-2026 it would no longer offer a paper-based test, with exact timelines varying by country; all tests move to computer, and some places will offer a "Writing on Paper" option. Make sure you book **Academic**, not General Training. [IELTS: changes to test delivery](https://ielts.org/news-and-insights/updates-to-ielts-test-delivery)
- **TOEFL iBT.** Taken on a computer at a test centre (a home edition also exists). Since 21 January 2026, score reports use a new 1–6 scale in half-point steps, aligned with CEFR levels. Many universities still publish requirements on the old 0–120 scale, and ETS publishes a comparison table, so check which scale each requirement uses. [ETS: score scale update](https://www.ets.org/toefl/institutions/ibt/score-scale-update.html)
- **Duolingo English Test.** About an hour, taken online with a webcam in a quiet private space, scored from 10 to 160. Duolingo says results arrive within two days and certified results can be sent to as many institutions as you like at no cost. Accepted by many US colleges and a growing list elsewhere. Not accepted everywhere, and not a UK visa SELT. [Duolingo: preparing for the test](https://blog.duolingo.com/duolingo-english-test-readiness/)

Most universities accept a score for **two years** from the test date, and ETS says TOEFL scores stay active for two years. Check that your score will still be valid on the day you apply, and on the day you enrol if the university checks it again.

## How results reach universities

- **IELTS:** results for IELTS on computer take 1–5 days. You can send results electronically to as many organisations as accept e-delivery, or send paper Test Report Forms to up to five organisations free, with a fee for more. Choose them in the booking portal or through your centre. [IELTS: getting and sharing results](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/your-results/getting-and-sharing-your-results)
- **TOEFL and Duolingo:** both send scores from your online account. Check each university's institution code or listing before you send.
- **Self-reporting:** some universities let you type your score into the application and send the official report later. Others want the official report before they review you. Note which is which on your list.

## How much preparation do you need?

A quick way to decide: take one full official practice test under timed conditions, then compare your result to the highest requirement on your list.

- **Already at or above the requirement:** one or two weeks of format practice is usually enough. Book a date soon.
- **Half a band (IELTS) below:** plan four to six weeks of focused practice on your weakest section, usually writing or speaking.
- **A full band or more below:** plan two to three months and book a date that still leaves time for a retake before your first deadline.

Writing and speaking improve fastest with feedback. Record yourself answering speaking questions, and get your essays marked against the official band descriptors, not by guessing.

## Step by step

1. List your universities and note their English rules in one place.
2. Decide which test covers the whole list, and whether any UK route needs a UKVI SELT.
3. Take an official practice test and decide how many weeks you need.
4. Search the provider's locator for your city and two possible dates.
5. Book through the official site with your passport name exactly as printed.
6. Prepare, then sit the test.
7. Send scores to each university the way it asks, and save the confirmation.
8. If you fall short, book the retake at once.

## A month-by-month plan for 2027 entry

This assumes you are in your final school year in Tashkent, Almaty, Bishkek or Dushanbe, and that most of your deadlines fall in January 2027.

- **September to October:** finish your university list, take a practice test, and book a date.
- **November:** sit the test. This leaves December for a retake if needed.
- **December:** retake if your score is short; send scores to universities with early-January deadlines.
- **January:** confirm each university has received the official score, not just your self-reported number.
- **Spring:** if an offer is conditional on English, check the deadline in the offer and whether your score will still be valid at enrolment.

If you are reading this later in the year, compress the plan: book first, then prepare in the weeks you have.

## Test-day basics

- Bring the exact ID you registered with, usually your passport. The name must match your booking.
- Arrive early. Late arrivals can be refused without a refund.
- For computer tests, practise on the official sample tests so the screen layout is not new on the day.
- For at-home Duolingo, test your camera, microphone and room setup the day before.

## Common mistakes

- Booking IELTS General Training instead of Academic.
- Assuming an at-home test will work for a UK visa.
- Reading a TOEFL requirement on the 0–120 scale and comparing it with a 1–6 score without the ETS table.
- Booking the last possible date before a deadline, with no time for a retake.
- A passport name that does not match the booking spelling, especially with transliteration from Cyrillic.
- Forgetting to send the official score after self-reporting it.

## Questions to ask each university

- Which English tests do you accept for my course, and what overall and section scores?
- Do you accept at-home or online versions?
- Will your English assessment count for the UK Student visa, or do I need a UKVI SELT?
- Do I need to send the official score before you review my application, or only before enrolment?
- Can my school's English-medium teaching replace a test? (Ask; do not assume.)

## Plan backwards from your first deadline

> **Example.** Aigerim in Almaty applies to UK courses with the 13 January 2027 deadline and to two US colleges with early-January deadlines. She lists what each accepts, sees IELTS is accepted everywhere on her list, and books a date that leaves room for a retake and for the score to be delivered before the earliest deadline. She also emails one UK university to ask whether her IELTS Academic result will be enough for the visa, and saves the answer.

Check the test provider's score-delivery time and each college's rules for official score reports. Leave room for one retake.

## Short answers

**Which test is "best"?** The one every university on your list accepts. There is no general winner.

**Can I mix tests?** Usually yes, as long as each university accepts the test you send it.

**Is one retake normal?** Yes. Plan for it from the start.
